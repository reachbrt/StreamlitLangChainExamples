# AI Research Team Knowledge Base

## Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique that enhances large language models by retrieving relevant information from external knowledge sources before generating responses. This approach helps ground the model's outputs in factual information, reducing hallucinations and improving accuracy.

### How RAG Works

1. **Indexing**: Documents are split into chunks and converted into vector embeddings.
2. **Retrieval**: When a query is received, the system finds the most relevant document chunks.
3. **Augmentation**: The retrieved information is added to the prompt sent to the language model.
4. **Generation**: The language model generates a response based on both the query and the retrieved information.

### Benefits of RAG

- Reduces hallucinations by grounding responses in factual information
- Allows models to access up-to-date information beyond their training data
- Improves transparency by citing sources
- Enables domain-specific knowledge without fine-tuning

### Implementation with LangChain

LangChain provides tools for implementing RAG systems:
- Text splitters for chunking documents
- Vector stores for efficient retrieval
- Embedding models for converting text to vectors
- Chain components for connecting retrieval with generation

## Our Research Focus

Our team is currently focused on several key areas of AI research:

### Conversational AI

We're developing more natural and context-aware conversational agents that can maintain coherent dialogues over extended interactions. Our work includes:
- Long-term memory mechanisms
- Contextual understanding
- Personality consistency
- Multi-turn reasoning

### Explainable AI

We believe AI systems should be transparent and understandable. Our research includes:
- Trace visualization techniques
- Step-by-step reasoning explanations
- Confidence metrics for generated content
- Source attribution methods

- Fair and unbiased
